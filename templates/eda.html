Key Insights

Observations obtained from exploratory analysis.

Traffic Condition

The dataset contains three traffic conditions: Low, Medium and High. These conditions are used as the target classes for traffic condition classification.

Traffic Priority

Traffic priority is represented using five levels from 1 to 5. The priority model predicts the appropriate level based on traffic parameters.

Traffic Density

Increasing traffic density generally represents a more congested traffic environment and can influence the predicted traffic condition.

Network Parameters

Signal strength and available bandwidth provide additional information about the communication environment of the vehicular network.
